Doppelganger Crowd

You are one face in a crowd of identical doppels. Do your errands. Act natural. Find the other humans by how they move.

Move: WASD / arrows (relative to the screen), or left-click a spot to walk there. C (hold) = stroll.
Dash: Shift — quick burst, 8s cooldown. Doppels rarely move that fast, so it can give you away.
Airstrike: aim with the mouse, press R. Costs 3 points, 40s cooldown. Everyone sees the warning for 2.5s before it lands: +5 per human hit, −1 per doppel hit.
Tag: hover a figure and press Space or right-click (must be close).
Task +1 · full set +2 · catch a human +5 · wrong tag −1 and a 6s lockout.
